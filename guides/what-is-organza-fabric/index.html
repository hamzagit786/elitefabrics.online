What Is Organza Fabric? The Sheer Structured Textile

What is organza fabric? Learn how sheer, crisp organza holds structural shapes in bridal wear and evening dresses, plus silk vs polyester varieties and care tips.

Organza fabric is a sheer, lightweight plain-weave textile known for its distinctive crisp hand, translucent shimmer, and sculpted volume in eveningwear and bridal gowns.
