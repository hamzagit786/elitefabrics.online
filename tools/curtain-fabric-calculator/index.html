Frequently Asked Questions

What fullness ratio should I use for curtains?

2.0x fullness is the standard for most pleated or rod-pocket draperies. Sheer voile curtains look best at 2.5x to 3.0x fullness for privacy and rich gathers. Heavier fabrics like velvet look luxurious at 2.0x to 2.2x.

How much extra length do I need for curtain hems?

Standard drapery construction requires 8 inches for a 4-inch double bottom hem and 4 inches for the top heading (12 inches total added to the finished drop).

What is wide-width curtain fabric?

Wide-width drapery fabric (108" to 118" wide) is designed to be turned sideways (railroaded) so you can make seamless curtains for wide windows without vertical joins.
