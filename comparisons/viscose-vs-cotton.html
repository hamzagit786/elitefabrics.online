Both cotton and viscose are composed of plant cellulose, making them naturally breathable and gentle against the skin. However, their physical structure creates stark differences: cotton is spun from short, natural staple fibers that yield a sturdy, comforting cloth that grows stronger when wet. Viscose is extruded into long, continuous filaments, producing a cooler, silkier, more fluid fabric that requires gentler care.
