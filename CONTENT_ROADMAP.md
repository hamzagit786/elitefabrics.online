# Elite Fabrics – Strategic Content Roadmap & Editorial Architecture

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Positioning
Elite Fabrics is positioned as an independent, non-commercial fabric and textile information authority. The site's primary objective is to capture high-intent, evergreen organic search queries from English-speaking and international textile markets (USA, UK, Europe, Pakistan, India, Australia, Canada) by providing rigorous, fact-checked, accessible textile education.

---

## 2. Topical Authority Clusters

### Cluster A: Fiber Science & Material Fundamentals (Evergreen Core)
*Target Audience: Fashion students, sewists, apparel designers, conscious consumers.*
*Primary Search Intent: Definitive, encyclopedic definitions with measurable physical attributes.*

1. **GSM (Grams per Square Meter) Demystified**
   - *Target Queries:* "what is GSM fabric", "how to choose fabric weight for clothing", "gsm chart for cotton and linen"
   - *Intent:* Informational / Practical
   - *Key Content:* Interactive or visual conversion table connecting GSM to garment categories (e.g., 80 GSM voile vs. 450 GSM denim).
   - *Priority:* Tier 1 (High volume, high backlink acquisition).

2. **Fibers vs. Weaves vs. Knits: The Fundamental Distinction**
   - *Target Queries:* "difference between fiber and weave", "is satin a fiber or weave", "knit vs woven fabric characteristics"
   - *Intent:* Clarification / Educational
   - *Key Content:* Dispel the common consumer confusion of conflating fiber (cotton/polyester) with structure (satin/twill).
   - *Priority:* Tier 1.

3. **Burn Testing & Microscopic Fiber Identification**
   - *Target Queries:* "how to test fabric with fire", "burn test for silk vs polyester", "natural vs synthetic fabric test"
   - *Intent:* Practical / DIY Verification
   - *Key Content:* Flame reaction, odor profile, ash residue comparison table, safety precautions.
   - *Priority:* Tier 1.

## 3. SEO Metadata & Structured Data Standards

For every new publication on Elite Fabrics:
1. **Title Tag:** Must follow format `[Entity/Topic]: [Core Characteristic / Benefit] | Elite Fabrics`. Under 60 characters.
2. **Meta Description:** Plain, informative English summarizing properties, GSM, fiber composition, or care. Under 155 characters.
3. **H1 Tag:** Exactly one clear, declarative H1 per view.
4. **Canonical URL:** Fully qualified URL matching `https://elitefabrics.online/...`.
5. **JSON-LD Schema Integration:**
   - `WebSite` & `Organization` on home and static pages.
   - `TechArticle` / `Article` on fabric profiles and blog articles.
   - `BreadcrumbList` on all detail and subsection views.
   - `FAQPage` strictly when matching visible accordion FAQs.
6. **Internal Linking Mandate:** Every new piece must receive at least 3 inbound links from existing hub pages and link out to at least 2 related fabric profiles and 1 comparison.

---

## 4. Editorial Integrity & Fact-Checking SOP
- Never cite unverified folklore as historical fact without explicit historical archival attribution.
- Avoid promotional language, corporate sponsor bias, or commercial shopping links.
- Use metric and imperial units (e.g., GSM and oz/yd²).
- Ensure plain-English explanations accessible to beginners without condescension.
