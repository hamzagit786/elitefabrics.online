Khaddar and Linen look surprisingly similar at first glance because both have a rustic, textured look with little bumps in the yarn. But they are made for opposite seasons! Linen is king of summer cooling, while Khaddar is woven thick to keep you cozy during dry winter months.

Verdict Guidance:

Choose **Khaddar** for cozy winter shalwar kameez, kurtas, shawls, and rustic home cushions. Choose **Linen** for beach holidays, summer shirts, light dresses, and breezy warm-weather outfits.

Detailed Head-to-Head Comparison

Which Season to Wear

Khaddar: Autumn and Winter; keeps body warmth trapped inside.

Linen: Spring and Summer; lets air circulate and cools you down.

Wearing heavy winter Khaddar on a hot July afternoon will make you sweat, while wearing light linen in December will leave you shivering.

What It Is Made Of

Khaddar: 100% thick, spun cotton yarn from local mills.

Linen: 100% plant fiber taken from flax stems.

Khaddar uses fluffy cotton yarns that hold in warm air. Linen uses smooth, strong flax fibers that release heat.

How It Wrinkles

Khaddar: Gentle, soft wrinkles that relax as you walk around.

Linen: Sharp creases that hold their shape.

Khaddar has a softer, heavier drape, while linen keeps crisp, breezy folds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get Khaddar for summer?

Yes! Artisans make a special "Summer Khaddar" or "Cotton Khaddar" made with finer, lighter yarns and an airy weave for warm spring and autumn days.
