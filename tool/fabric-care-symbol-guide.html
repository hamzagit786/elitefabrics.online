Frequently Asked Questions

What do the dots inside the wash tub symbol mean?

Dots inside the wash tub represent maximum water temperature: 1 dot = 30°C (85°F Cold), 2 dots = 40°C (105°F Warm), 3 dots = 50°C (120°F Hot), 4 dots = 60°C (140°F Very Hot), 5 dots = 70°C (160°F), and 6 dots = 95°C (200°F Boil wash).

What do the bars under laundry symbols mean?

Bars under any care symbol indicate reduced mechanical agitation and gentler handling: No bar = Normal cycle; 1 bar = Permanent Press / Mild mechanical action; 2 bars = Gentle / Delicate cycle with minimal agitation and slow spin.
