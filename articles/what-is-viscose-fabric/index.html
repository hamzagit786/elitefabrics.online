What Is Viscose Fabric? The Silk Alternative Explained

What is viscose fabric? Discover how viscous wood cellulose is transformed into fluid, breathable, silk-like cloth with washing rules and styling advice.

Viscose fabric is a silky regenerated cellulose textile created using the viscose process. Renowned for its fluid drape and skin cooling, it is a staple of modern fashion.
